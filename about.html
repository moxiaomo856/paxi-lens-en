About Paxi Lens

Paxi Lens is a non-custodial toolkit for the Paxi ecosystem: 20 on-chain tools (decoder, token launch, swap, governance, delegation, burn, and more) are all integrated into this single page. It is a pure front-end, and private keys never leave your wallet.

The built-in "On-Chain Decoder" is a lightweight decoding module that turns raw transaction, contract, and wallet data into plain language. It is read-only and requires no wallet connection.

Paxi Lens is not an investment and not a custodian. Service fees go 100% to a single transparent address, and you use it at your own risk.
